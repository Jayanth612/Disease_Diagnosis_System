# VitaScan — test log (before vs after fix)

Issues found during manual testing, with the same inputs rescored after
retraining (raw-value scaling bundles + SMOTE) and UI/API fixes.

Root causes fixed:
- Models were trained on StandardScaler features, but the app sent raw values
- Stroke / hepatitis class imbalance (SMOTE on train split)
- Hepatitis form used wrong lab units (ALB g/L, CHOL mmol/L, CREA µmol/L, etc.)
- UI sent broken one-hots (residence/smoking) and mixed fields across diseases
